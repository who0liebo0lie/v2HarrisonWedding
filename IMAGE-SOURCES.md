# Image Source and Attribution Log

## Existing approved project assets
All Julia and Wesley portraits, page artwork, floral artwork, ship-map artwork, and neighborhood photographs were already present in the supplied v24 project and were retained as approved user-provided/project assets.

## v25 derived assets
Files in `assets/venues/` are local crops derived from the existing approved neighborhood photographs. They were created to stop exact image repetition in the interface. They should be treated as temporary visual placeholders, not verified photographs of the named restaurant or venue.

## Official research references
Venue names and descriptions were checked against official Royal Caribbean Harmony of the Seas and dining pages, including Royal Caribbean's Harmony of the Seas “Things to Do” page and Café Promenade page.

No Instagram or TikTok images were scraped, hotlinked, or downloaded. No new third-party copyrighted venue photographs were included because reliable reuse permission was not established during this revision.

Before public launch, replace temporary venue crops with photographs licensed for website use and record each final source and permission here.
