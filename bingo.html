How this page gets populated: guests submit a fun fact when they RSVP and opt in to Bingo (see the RSVP page). Those facts are collected in data/bingo-facts.json — Julia builds the live bingo card by copying facts from that file (or the linked spreadsheet, if you prefer to manage it there) into the card grid below before the cruise sets sail. Full steps are in BINGO-SETUP.md.

How it works

1. RSVP

RSVP to the cruise and opt in to Bingo — you're in!

3. Your bingo card

As facts come in, you'll get your very own digital bingo card.

4. The game begins

Once we set sail, the game goes live.

5. Match, connect, win

Match facts to the right people, climb the leaderboard, and earn bragging rights.
