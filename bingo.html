Harrison Set SailBingo

A virtual, personalized bingo game made from the fun facts your fellow guests share when they RSVP. Meet people, solve clues, and see who reaches the top of the leaderboard.

Virtual BoardsPersonalized CluesBragging Rights Only

How It Works

1️⃣

Share a Fun Fact

Guests who opt into Bingo submit one fun fact or clue with their RSVP.

2️⃣

Get Your Board

After RSVPs are collected, each participating guest receives a personalized virtual board.

3️⃣

Play During the Cruise

Match clues to guests, mark your board, and follow the leaderboard throughout the sailing.

Good to Know

  • No paper cards are required.
  • The game goes live after RSVP fun facts are submitted.
  • Boards are personalized, so guest experiences may differ.
  • There are no prizes—only excellent bragging rights.
