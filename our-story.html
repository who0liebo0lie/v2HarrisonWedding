Life pulled Wesley and Julia in different directions, but some connections don’t expire.

After 15 years of not talking, on May 28th Wesley sent a single text just to see how Julia was doing. That one message reopened a door that had never fully closed.

From there it was art museums and long conversations, mountains and jungles, and slow mornings by the beach. The two of them discovered that a friendship begun two decades ago had quietly been waiting to become something bigger.

Now they’re inviting the people they love to celebrate what twenty years and one brave text finally became.
