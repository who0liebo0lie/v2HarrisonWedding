A mutual friend introduced them twenty years ago and had no idea what they were starting.

What did Julia say? Wesley had to look it up too. Years earlier, he had done a lot of volunteer work in Russian, but it had been many years since he had used it regularly. Julia's first reply meant: "New phone, who dis?"

And then the story continues...

Life pulled Wesley and Julia in different directions, but some connections don't expire. After years of not talking, on May 28th Wesley sent a single text just to see how Julia was doing. That one message reopened a door that had never fully closed.

From there it was art museums and long conversations, mountains and jungles, and slow mornings by the beach. The two of them discovered that a friendship begun two decades ago had quietly been waiting to become something bigger.

Now they're inviting the people they love to celebrate what twenty years and one brave text finally became. ♡
