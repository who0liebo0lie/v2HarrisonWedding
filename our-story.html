Our Story

Hey, Julia…
Новый телефон, кто это?

What did Julia say?

Wesley had to look it up too. Years earlier he had done a lot of volunteer work in Russian. He had not been using it regularly. Julia's first reply meant “New phone, who dis?”

And then the story continues

One message reopened a door that had never fully closed. What followed were long conversations, shared memories, art museums, beach mornings, deep talks, and plenty of laughter. A friendship that began years earlier slowly grew into the love story that brought them here.

Now they're inviting the people they love to celebrate what twenty years and one brave text finally became.
