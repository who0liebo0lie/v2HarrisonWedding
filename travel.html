Understanding Cruise Pricing

The prices shown represent the highest price we expect guests to pay per person through the negotiated group.

Royal Caribbean routinely offers deals. When a lower eligible rate is available at booking, guests receive the lower price—the fare will not exceed the listed negotiated maximum.

Booking FAQs

How do I book my cabin?

Use the Aligned Journeys booking link or contact the agency by email or phone once booking opens.

Can the price be lower?

Yes. The listed amount is the negotiated maximum; eligible Royal Caribbean promotions may reduce your actual fare.

Is a deposit required?

Yes. A small deposit is needed to reserve a cabin.

Can I upgrade later?

Upgrades depend on availability and current pricing. Ask Aligned Journeys for assistance.
