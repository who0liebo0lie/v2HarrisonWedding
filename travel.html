Understanding Cruise Pricing

The prices shown on this website represent the maximum expected price per person when booking through our group, based on double occupancy.

Royal Caribbean routinely offers promotions. Depending on when you book, your actual fare may be significantly lower than the prices shown.

Aligned Journeys will help apply the best available group rate and eligible promotions.

What’s Included

Accommodations, many meals, entertainment, pools, Broadway-style shows, ice shows, AquaTheater productions, youth programs, and fitness center access.

Usually Not Included

Specialty dining, spa services, alcoholic beverages, casino play, shore excursions, and internet packages.
