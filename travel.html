Aligned Journeys will help our guests reserve their rooms and answer booking questions along the way.

Your negotiated rate

The listed negotiated rate is the highest you will pay. If Royal Caribbean advertises a lower fare at the time of booking, you will receive the lower price instead.

A small deposit holds your stateroom. Final payment will be due in October. Exact room categories and maximum per-person rates can be added here as soon as they are finalized.

Need help?

Booking FAQ

How do I reserve a cabin?

Use the Aligned Journeys booking link once it is activated. The link can be added without changing this page design.

What if Royal Caribbean has a lower price?

You receive the lower publicly available fare and will never pay more than the negotiated maximum.

When is final payment due?

Final payment is due in October. The exact date will be posted here when confirmed.
